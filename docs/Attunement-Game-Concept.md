# Attunement — An MMORPG of Discovery, Power \& Character Evolution

## Game Overview

**Attunement** is a persistent-world multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) centered on character development, exploration, specialization, discovery and deeply interconnected progression systems.

Drawing inspiration from **Anarchy Online** and **World of Warcraft**, the game combines the intricate character-building, equipment dependencies, implants, augmentations and skill interactions of Anarchy Online with the class identity, ability progression, world exploration, social gameplay, dungeons and raids of World of Warcraft.

At its heart is a simple premise:

**Your character is not defined solely by their class or level, but by what they discover, the powers they master, the modifications they install and the choices they make.**

Players inhabit a persistent fictional world filled with competing factions, ancient knowledge, advanced technologies, powerful creatures, hidden locations and opportunities for cooperation, competition and discovery.

The world and its systems are designed to remain compelling even with a small community of approximately 5, 10 or 25 players. A larger population may enrich the experience, but the game's fundamental appeal, progression and opportunities must not depend on hundreds or thousands of concurrent participants.

Character development extends beyond conventional experience points and equipment upgrades. Players can discover glyphs, develop attunements, install augmentations, master abilities and uncover interactions between seemingly unrelated systems.

The result is an MMORPG where two characters of the same class and level can possess radically different capabilities, specializations and playstyles.

## 1\. The World — Technology, Ancient Power and Discovery

The game takes place in a world where advanced technology, mysterious forces and ancient civilizations intersect.

Forgotten ruins conceal powerful glyphs. Abandoned facilities contain experimental augmentation technologies. Competing factions seek control over resources, knowledge and territories. Ancient powers influence the environment, its inhabitants and those capable of becoming attuned to them.

Science and supernatural forces need not be opposing systems. They may interact, compete or reveal deeper connections as players explore the world.

The setting supports a mixture of science fiction and fantasy, incorporating advanced machinery, biological modification, energy-based abilities, arcane phenomena, ancient artifacts and strange forms of life.

The world is not simply a collection of zones arranged by level. It contains secrets, opportunities, dangers and discoveries that may remain relevant throughout a character's lifetime.

Some locations require preparation, cooperation or particular capabilities to access. Others reward experimentation, observation or returning with new knowledge.

The world should feel inhabited and responsive regardless of how many players are present. Persistent settlements, factions, creatures, environmental events and evolving conditions provide continuity without depending on a constantly populated server.

Discovery is as much a part of advancement as defeating enemies.

## 2\. Character Creation, Identity and Classes

Players begin by creating a character with a distinct identity, appearance, origin and initial specialization.

Classes provide recognizable foundations for combat capabilities and playstyle identities, drawing from traditional MMORPG archetypes while allowing substantially different interpretations of their functions.

The fundamental archetypes include:

- **Defenders and tanks:** Characters specializing in protection, damage mitigation, interception, threat management, battlefield control or preventing enemies from reaching vulnerable allies.
- **Healers and restorers:** Characters responsible for recovery, regeneration, stabilization and sustaining allies, potentially through conventional healing, biotechnology, restorative machines, energy manipulation, symbiotic organisms or other unconventional approaches.
- **Damage specialists:** Characters emphasizing offensive capabilities through melee combat, ranged weapons, technology, summoned entities, environmental manipulation, supernatural powers or sustained damage mechanisms.
- **Support specialists:** Characters improving group effectiveness through buffs, debuffs, resource management, tactical coordination, crowd control, companion abilities, environmental interactions or specialized utility.
- **Controllers and disruptors:** Characters manipulating movement, enemy behavior, positioning, ability availability, environmental conditions or the tactical flow of an encounter.
- **Hybrid specialists:** Characters combining aspects of multiple archetypes, sometimes sacrificing maximum performance in one area for flexibility, unusual combinations or situational advantages.

These archetypes describe fundamental gameplay functions, not mandatory class names, appearances, equipment choices or fixed combat rotations.

**A healer need not resemble a traditional fantasy priest. A tank need not carry a shield. A support character need not remain at the back of the group.**

A heavily armored engineer might protect allies through deployable barriers, automated interception systems and mechanical companions.

A healer might operate through biological symbiosis, transferring regenerative organisms between allies or altering the environment to support recovery.

A defensive caster might survive by redirecting damage, manipulating enemy attention or controlling space rather than wearing heavy armor.

An offensive support specialist might increase group survivability by disrupting enemy capabilities rather than restoring lost health.

The intended role should remain understandable, but its presentation and execution can challenge genre conventions.

### Example class direction — Symbiotech Support Specialist

One distinctive class archetype is a support-oriented specialist capable of developing, deploying and coordinating technological, biological or hybrid companions.

Their companion options could include:

- **Technological companions:** Drones, autonomous constructs, robotic assistants, defensive platforms, repair units and specialized combat machines.
- **Symbiotechnological companions:** Engineered organisms, biomechanical constructs, living implants, symbiotic entities and hybrid companions combining artificial systems with biological functions.
- **Specialized support companions:** Entities designed for healing, protection, disruption, reconnaissance, resource regeneration or tactical manipulation.

These companions are not necessarily conventional combat pets that follow the player and attack enemies.

Some may be stationary, deployed temporarily, attached to allies, integrated into equipment or capable of independent tactical behavior.

A symbiotech companion might attach itself to an injured ally, providing regenerative support while altering the ally's defensive characteristics.

A mechanical companion might intercept incoming attacks, construct temporary cover or redistribute energy between group members.

A hybrid organism could interact with the character's attunements, changing its function according to installed augmentations or acquired glyphs.

Companion selection, specialization and configuration can meaningfully influence the class's role.

One player might develop into a dedicated companion-based healer. Another might focus on battlefield control, defensive support or damage amplification. A third might pursue a hybrid arrangement involving multiple specialized companions.

The class should reward tactical decisions, positioning, preparation and interaction with companions rather than merely requiring the player to maintain a collection of automated units.

Companions can also offer distinctive exploration, utility and noncombat capabilities.

### Class identity and freedom

A class establishes a foundation rather than an absolute limitation.

Characters develop through interconnected choices involving:

- Attributes and skills.
- Class abilities and specialization.
- Equipment and weapon proficiencies.
- Implants and augmentations.
- Attunements and affinities.
- Glyphs, runes and discovered powers.
- Perks, talents and passive abilities.
- Companion development and configurations.
- Faction relationships and world discoveries.

Class identity remains meaningful. A heavily armored front-line specialist should feel different from a support-oriented technician, symbiotic healer or energy-manipulating caster.

At the same time, individual character development allows considerable variation within each class.

**The goal is to preserve recognizable gameplay roles while allowing different ways to fulfill them.**

## 3\. Attributes, Skills and Character Development

Character advancement combines traditional RPG leveling with more intricate skill-based progression.

Levels represent broad advancement and open access to abilities, specializations and content. Skills and attributes provide more granular control over how characters develop.

Inspired particularly by Anarchy Online, character development involves decisions about where to invest limited resources and which capabilities to prioritize.

Attributes can influence multiple skills, equipment requirements and abilities. Equipment, buffs, implants, companions and other modifications may temporarily or permanently change the character's effective capabilities.

This creates opportunities for careful character planning and optimization.

A player might discover that a specific combination of equipment, implants and temporary enhancements allows them to use an otherwise inaccessible item.

Such achievements should feel like satisfying demonstrations of system mastery rather than tedious exercises in navigating obscure requirements.

Character progression therefore rewards both participation and understanding.

## 4\. Attunement — Affinity with Powers and Disciplines

Attunement represents a character's developing connection to particular sources of power, disciplines, forces or phenomena within the game world.

These connections may arise through exploration, combat encounters, faction interactions, quests, artifacts, rituals or exposure to unusual environments.

Attunement is more than a numerical progression track.

It may influence which abilities a character can access, how existing powers behave, which entities recognize them and what opportunities become available.

For example, a character exploring ancient energy installations might develop an affinity with a particular form of resonance.

That affinity could allow them to interact with previously inaccessible devices, discover specialized glyphs or modify certain abilities.

Characters may develop multiple attunements, but pursuing one affinity could create trade-offs or interactions with another.

Some affinities might be complementary. Others could be unstable, mutually exclusive or capable of producing unexpected combinations.

Attunement creates an additional dimension of character identity without replacing traditional class progression.

## 5\. Glyphs, Runes and the Discovery of Abilities

Glyphs are discoverable symbols, inscriptions or encoded manifestations of power that interact with character abilities.

Drawing inspiration from World of Warcraft's glyph systems and rune discovery, glyphs offer more than ordinary ability upgrades.

Depending on their properties, glyphs may:

- Modify the behavior of an existing ability.
- Introduce an alternative effect or interaction.
- Enable an unusual specialization.
- Change damage types, targeting or utility.
- Unlock a passive effect or conditional capability.
- Interact with particular attunements, companions or augmentations.
- Reveal additional discoveries when combined with other elements.

A glyph might transform a single-target energy attack into a chaining attack, add a defensive property to a movement ability or change how a healing technique interacts with nearby allies.

For a symbiotech specialist, a glyph might change a companion's primary function, allowing a regenerative organism to provide protection instead of healing, or enabling a mechanical drone to redistribute beneficial effects between allies.

Some glyphs may be discovered through exploration or rare encounters. Others might emerge from crafting, research, faction rewards or completing demanding challenges.

Not every glyph needs to be powerful. Some may offer situational utility, cosmetic transformations or unusual combinations.

The attraction lies in discovering what a glyph does, understanding its possibilities and experimenting with how it interacts with a character's build.

## 6\. Implants, Augmentations and Character Modification

Augmentation forms one of the game's deeper character-development systems.

Inspired by the implants, clusters, symbiants, nanoprograms and equipment dependencies of Anarchy Online, augmentation allows players to modify their characters through technological, biological or supernatural enhancements.

Characters can acquire specialized modifications that influence attributes, skills, abilities, resistances, companion functions or other capabilities.

An augmentation might strengthen a particular skill, introduce a unique effect or alter how existing equipment functions.

Different modifications may compete for installation capacity, require certain attributes or interact with other installed components.

Players must make decisions about which enhancements best support their intended specialization.

More sophisticated augmentations can offer multiple benefits but also introduce trade-offs.

An enhancement increasing offensive capability might reduce defensive efficiency. Another could improve a character's interaction with certain glyphs while weakening an unrelated affinity.

Augmentation should enable experimentation, creative optimization and distinctive builds rather than simply providing a linear sequence of increasingly powerful upgrades.

## 7\. Interconnected Systems and Build Crafting

The game's defining mechanical depth comes from the relationships between its progression systems.

Character classes, attributes, skills, equipment, attunements, glyphs, companions and augmentations are interconnected rather than operating as isolated features.

A character's equipment might improve a skill that satisfies an augmentation requirement. That augmentation could enhance a class ability, which in turn behaves differently when combined with a particular glyph or attunement.

These interactions create opportunities for players to discover builds that are not immediately obvious.

For example:

**The Resonant Guardian**

A defensive character combines protective equipment with an energy-based attunement. An installed augmentation converts part of incoming damage into stored energy, while a discovered glyph allows the character to release that energy as a protective effect for nearby allies.

**The Phase Engineer**

A technical specialist develops mobility-related abilities and acquires a rare augmentation that modifies short-range teleportation. A compatible glyph changes the ability to affect a deployed device, opening alternative ways to position equipment during encounters.

**The Symbiotic Adept**

A support-focused character uses biological enhancements, restorative abilities and an attunement associated with living systems. Particular combinations strengthen sustained group support while introducing vulnerabilities that require careful positioning and resource management.

**The Symbiotech Conductor**

A companion-oriented specialist coordinates technological drones and engineered symbiotic organisms. One companion provides protection, while another supports allies through regeneration or resource transfer. Glyphs and augmentations alter companion interactions, creating opportunities for defensive, restorative or disruptive specializations.

**The Inversion Healer**

A restorative specialist uses an unconventional healing system that manipulates damage distribution, converts certain harmful effects into regenerative resources or temporarily binds allies together through a shared survival mechanism. Its strength depends on positioning, timing and understanding the group's condition rather than repeatedly casting conventional healing spells.

These examples illustrate how different systems can interact to create recognizable playstyles.

The objective is not unlimited complexity, but **meaningful combinations that produce new tactical possibilities**.

Build crafting becomes a substantial part of the game experience, rewarding curiosity, experimentation and system knowledge.

## 8\. Combat, Abilities and Tactical Encounters

Combat draws from traditional MMORPG systems, emphasizing class roles, active abilities, positioning, resource management and cooperation.

Characters engage enemies through combinations of weapon attacks, class abilities, specialized equipment, companion commands, glyph-modified powers and other capabilities.

Different builds influence tactical choices rather than simply determining damage output.

Encounters may reward crowd control, defensive preparation, mobility, interrupt abilities, support coordination, resistance management or understanding environmental conditions.

A character optimized for direct damage may perform exceptionally in one encounter but struggle where utility or survivability is more important.

### Flexible role expression

Combat supports familiar archetypal functions without prescribing conventional appearances or identical gameplay patterns.

A healer might restore health directly, prevent damage from occurring, manage shared recovery resources or manipulate companion-based regenerative systems.

A tank might withstand attacks, redirect them, manipulate enemy positioning or prevent dangerous actions through technological or supernatural interference.

A support specialist might operate at close range, control multiple deployed units, maintain a network of effects or temporarily change the capabilities of allies.

Characters may also change their contribution dynamically during encounters depending on equipment, abilities, companions or circumstances.

**The function of a role should be recognizable, while its mechanics, appearance and combat rhythm remain open to creative interpretation.**

Combat should accommodate both approachable playstyles and mechanically demanding approaches, allowing players to participate without mastering every underlying progression system.

## 9\. Exploration, Quests and World Discovery

Exploration is a major component of the game.

The world contains settlements, wilderness regions, forgotten cities, research facilities, underground complexes, dangerous territories and areas influenced by unusual powers.

Players encounter quests, faction conflicts, environmental mysteries, rare creatures and hidden discoveries.

Some opportunities become available through conventional progression. Others depend on exploration, accumulated knowledge, attunement, equipment or particular character capabilities.

A sealed research station might require a technological specialization, an acquired access credential or an alternative route discovered through exploration.

An ancient structure might respond differently depending on the player's attunement.

A hidden glyph could be associated with a series of encounters that gradually reveal the history of a forgotten civilization.

Companions, specialized tools and unusual character abilities may provide additional ways to interact with the environment.

Exploration should remain satisfying for individual players, small groups and the wider community alike.

These discoveries give the world a sense of history and depth while providing motivations beyond leveling.

## 10\. Multiplayer, Guilds and Social Progression

As a persistent online RPG, shared-world interaction is a central part of the experience.

However, the game is conceived around a potentially small, close-knit player community rather than requiring conventional large-scale MMO population levels.

A world with five active players should still offer meaningful individual adventures, progression and opportunities for cooperation.

With ten players, group compositions, specializations, trading relationships and shared discoveries can create additional social depth.

With twenty-five players, more varied guild activities, cooperative encounters, faction interactions and player specializations become possible.

These numbers describe illustrative community sizes, not fixed population limits or guaranteed concurrent player counts. The game can accommodate a larger population while preserving its underlying identity.

### Meaningful cooperation at small scale

Players encounter one another while exploring, completing quests, fighting enemies, gathering resources, crafting equipment and visiting settlements.

Cooperative activities include parties, dungeons, challenging encounters, shared exploration, guild objectives and world events.

Group content should account for different numbers of available participants, including individual players, small parties and larger organized groups.

The experience must not depend on filling conventional raid rosters or maintaining a particular distribution of healers, tanks and damage dealers.

Flexible class specializations, alternative encounter strategies, companion systems and adjustable encounter structures can create opportunities for cooperation without making absent players a barrier to participation.

A small group should still experience tactical coordination and meaningful role interactions.

Larger groups can introduce additional encounter mechanics, strategic complexity and opportunities for specialization rather than merely increasing enemy health and damage.

### Guild identity

Guilds provide persistent social structures for shared activities, resources, events and collective achievements.

A guild of five committed players should be capable of establishing a recognizable identity, developing shared accomplishments and influencing its surroundings.

Knowledge itself becomes socially valuable.

Experienced players can share information, mentor newcomers and develop community understanding of complex mechanics.

A small population can strengthen the significance of individual reputations, discoveries and contributions.

Guilds and groups support casual participation and organized challenging content without requiring demanding schedules or a large recruitment pool.

## 11\. Crafting, Equipment and Player Economy

Equipment is an important part of character identity and progression.

Weapons, armor, devices, implants, companion components, enhancement materials and rare resources can be acquired through combat, exploration, crafting and trade.

Crafting goes beyond creating conventional equipment.

Players may construct augmentation components, refine materials, assemble specialized devices, develop companion enhancements or experiment with combinations that influence item properties.

Some recipes are widely known, while others are discovered through exploration, faction relationships or unusual materials.

A player-driven economy allows individuals to develop identities as crafters, traders, resource gatherers or specialist equipment suppliers.

### An economy that functions with few players

The economy should remain useful without depending on a large, continuously active marketplace.

Players can acquire essential resources and equipment through multiple routes, including exploration, personal crafting, NPC merchants, faction services and cooperative exchange.

Player specialization and trading offer meaningful advantages without making another player's availability a prerequisite for ordinary advancement.

Rare discoveries and specialized craftsmanship can still carry substantial value within a small community.

As the population grows, greater specialization and more sophisticated trading relationships can develop naturally.

Economic depth should emerge from interesting materials, equipment dependencies, crafting decisions and resource choices rather than artificial scarcity caused by insufficient participants.

## 12\. Dungeons, Raids and Challenging Content

Group content combines the encounter design and cooperative roles associated with World of Warcraft with the character specialization and preparation characteristic of Anarchy Online.

Dungeons provide structured challenges for individuals or smaller groups, incorporating combat encounters, environmental interactions, exploration and rewards.

Larger cooperative encounters introduce more demanding mechanics, specialized responsibilities and tactical coordination.

### Encounter scale and flexibility

The game's encounter philosophy should support a community that may consist of approximately 5, 10 or 25 players in total, potentially expanding beyond that.

Not every player will necessarily be online or available simultaneously.

Consequently, the experience should not rely on conventional fixed-size raid requirements.

An encounter might support several approaches depending on participation:

- A lone player using careful preparation, companions, environmental mechanics and an appropriate specialization.
- A small party coordinating defensive, restorative, offensive and support capabilities.
- A larger group engaging additional mechanics, simultaneous objectives or more complex tactical interactions.

Not every encounter needs to support every configuration. Some should reward genuine cooperation and cannot necessarily be completed alone.

However, unavailable group members should not render the broader game experience empty or prevent meaningful character development.

### Preparation and mastery

Preparation can involve equipment configuration, resistance management, consumables, companion selection, attunements and appropriate ability combinations.

Certain encounters may reveal alternative strategies depending on the group's composition and available capabilities.

Success can reward rare equipment, augmentation components, glyph discoveries, faction reputation and access to new areas.

The game emphasizes meaningful preparation and mastery without making obscure prerequisites, excessive repetition or large-group attendance requirements the primary challenge.

Raids represent coordinated, substantial encounters rather than being defined solely by a particular participant count.

## 13\. PvP and Faction Conflict

Player-versus-player combat introduces another dimension to character specialization.

Competitive encounters may occur through duels, small-group combat, faction-controlled regions, objectives or designated conflict areas.

Build diversity creates tactical variation, where mobility, defense, disruption, burst damage and support capabilities each have distinct value.

Faction conflicts can influence territories, resources, access to services and world events.

With a small player population, competitive gameplay should remain meaningful without requiring large, evenly populated factions or constantly available opponents.

Faction activity can involve persistent world objectives, NPC-controlled opposition, strategic decisions and occasional direct player conflict.

PvP should provide meaningful competition without rendering noncompetitive character development irrelevant.

Character customization remains important, but effective balance requires avoiding combinations that make particular builds overwhelmingly dominant.

## 14\. Character Evolution and Long-Term Identity

Progression does not end when a character reaches a conventional level cap.

A character continues developing through equipment optimization, new specializations, rare discoveries, faction relationships, augmentation combinations, companion evolution, crafting expertise and challenging encounters.

Horizontal progression is as important as numerical advancement.

A newly discovered glyph might not increase a character's overall power but could enable a fundamentally different combat approach.

An augmentation might make an established ability useful in an unfamiliar situation.

A new attunement might unlock interactions with parts of the world that previously seemed inaccessible.

A companion might undergo a transformation that changes its appearance, tactical function or relationship with its character.

Characters can develop recognizable identities through their choices, achievements, appearances, equipment, companions and specializations.

In a small community, these identities can become particularly meaningful. Individual characters may be remembered for unusual builds, exceptional crafting abilities, rare discoveries, distinctive companions or their contributions to challenging encounters.

The objective is to make long-term character investment rewarding without reducing the experience to endless statistical inflation.

## 15\. The Core Gameplay Loop

The MMORPG experience follows an interconnected cycle:

1. **Explore:** Travel through the world, encounter threats, meet players, discover locations and pursue quests.
2. **Fight and cooperate:** Engage in combat, dungeons, challenging encounters, world events and faction conflicts.
3. **Advance:** Gain experience, improve skills, acquire equipment and develop class abilities.
4. **Discover:** Find glyphs, rare materials, hidden knowledge, attunements and unusual modifications.
5. **Customize:** Combine equipment, implants, augmentations, companions, abilities and specializations into distinctive builds.
6. **Experiment:** Test different combinations, investigate synergies and adapt to different challenges.
7. **Master:** Overcome more demanding encounters, deepen specializations and establish a unique character identity.

Progression continually feeds back into exploration and player choice.

A stronger character can face new challenges. A more specialized character may discover alternative solutions. A newly acquired capability can give familiar content a different meaning.

The loop remains rewarding whether the player adventures alone, cooperates with a few familiar companions or participates in a larger community activity.

## 16\. Game Identity and Design Philosophy

The game draws from two complementary MMORPG traditions.

**From Anarchy Online:** Deep skill systems, character optimization, implants, symbiants, equipment requirements, buffs, complex dependencies, specialized professions, technological and biological themes, companion-oriented gameplay and the satisfaction of understanding interconnected mechanics.

**From World of Warcraft:** Distinctive class identities, responsive ability-based combat, recognizable combat archetypes, exploration, questing, glyphs, rune discovery, attunements, talent choices, group content, raids and shared-world experiences.

The combination creates an MMORPG where mechanical depth and world discovery reinforce one another.

The game does not need to recreate either inspiration's exact mechanics. Its identity comes from bringing together their strongest character-development principles within a cohesive fictional universe.

### Distinctive principles

**Recognizable roles, unconventional expressions.** Tanks, healers, damage dealers, support specialists and hybrids remain important, but their appearance, abilities and combat flow can differ substantially from genre expectations.

**Companions as meaningful capabilities.** Technological, biological and symbiotechnological companions can function as extensions of class identity, combat specialization, support capabilities and player choice rather than simply additional sources of damage.

**Depth without mandatory complexity.** Players can engage with the game at different levels of mechanical understanding, from straightforward character development to intricate build optimization.

**A persistent world without population dependence.** The game retains its appeal with a handful of players. Larger communities create additional opportunities rather than supplying the minimum population required for the experience to function.

**Cooperation without rigid group dependency.** Teamwork remains rewarding, and certain challenges genuinely benefit from multiple players, but ordinary advancement and world engagement do not depend on assembling conventional MMORPG group sizes.

**Discovery with consequences.** Finding something unusual should matter. A rare glyph, augmentation, attunement or companion modification can change what a character can do and how they approach the world.

**Horizontal and vertical advancement.** Increased power remains satisfying, but new capabilities, combinations, specializations and tactical possibilities are equally important.

**Class distinction without unnecessary restriction.** Different classes have identifiable strengths and weaknesses, while individual builds can significantly alter how those strengths are expressed.

**A world worth inhabiting.** Exploration, atmosphere, lore, environmental storytelling, faction interactions, persistent locations and memorable encounters matter independently of player population.

Complexity should serve decisions, not exist for its own sake. Discovery should produce genuine surprises. Character choices should matter. Specialized builds should offer different capabilities rather than merely different numerical outputs.

Above all, the game should preserve the satisfaction of **becoming capable of something that was previously impossible**, whether through increased power, a rare discovery, a carefully constructed character build or cooperation with other players.

## Defining Vision

**Attunement is a persistent-world MMORPG about building a character, discovering a world and mastering the relationships between its systems.**

Players are not merely accumulating levels and acquiring stronger equipment.

They are discovering capabilities, developing specializations, uncovering hidden interactions, experimenting with modifications and shaping characters whose abilities reflect a long history of exploration and decisions.

Its familiar MMORPG archetypes remain meaningful but are not confined to familiar expressions. Healers might command regenerative organisms, defenders might manipulate space and incoming damage, and support specialists might coordinate networks of technological and symbiotechnological companions.

Its world is intended to feel consequential whether shared by five players, ten, twenty-five or a much larger community.

**The scale of the population does not define the scale of the adventure.**

A world in which discovery matters, knowledge carries value, player cooperation creates opportunities and character-building is an adventure in itself.
