# Kestrel Deep — containment and research recovery

A surviving Quarantine Custodian is still enforcing the facility's old safety perimeter. It patrols the covered chamber on a five-point circuit, attacks on proximity and telegraphs Containment Sweep before a local discharge. Its three-strut mechanical chassis, asymmetric tool arm and recessed sensor are distinct from the wildlife and relay sentinel. The Kestrel Phase Recorder is a dedicated interactive asset at the rear of the chamber.

The recorder reveals a local research fragment, then grants one Kestrel Phase Damper to a Resonance-attuned visitor who already owns Sinter Laminate and is out of combat. The reference sample is not consumed. The implant is a sidegrade (+30 HP, -10% outgoing damage), not an additional slot or automatic equip. Avoiding or defeating the custodian is a tactical choice, not a persistent kill flag. Existing unique item ownership prevents duplicate rewards and no new save schema is introduced.

The new laboratory walls explicitly block hostile ability initiation, auto/companion attacks, damage and initial aggro along intersecting segments. The custodian drops obstructed threat. This is a scoped 2D segment/rectangle check for Kestrel's three walls, not a general cover/visibility or vertical combat model. Healing and buffs retain the existing planar semantics. Design rationale: do not add enclosed rooms while leaving their new containment barriers entirely meaningless.

Simulated review: game design — a physical industrial obstacle and optional cross-biome progression, one encounter with readable windup; narrative — abandoned infrastructure retains recognizable purpose, no definitive lore origin; art — unique mechanical silhouette and terminal, restrained emissions, no new assets download; security — server verifies inventory, attunement, defeat, proximity, wall and combat state, no arbitrary client grant; adversarial — patrol, both directions of structural wall checks, no cooldown spend or damage on blocked direct ability, duplicate item, no reference consumption; retrospective — precede stacked floors, full visibility/pathfinding and further research trees with a validated bounded room. Browser captures are evidence of rendering, not artistic acceptance or measured play balance.


## Consolidated reconciliation
Direct hostile actions, threat acquisition and companion attacks retain the existing general hasLineOfSight gate. Kestrel wall-only damage defense is retained at the lower-level damage API with explicit impact origins for relocated Echo, remote rig and ground specials. This preserves damage credited to a caster outside a local impact area. No second broad LOS rule or owner-to-companion target restriction is added. Recorder uses general cover. Browser uses the already earned Surveyor sample, with fixture kill explicitly identified; real encounter behavior is covered by Node tests.
