# Sinter Flats — a bounded ecological biome

A southern wind-scoured mineral bed (6, 58; radius 16), in the existing Verdigris Reach. Distinct pale, desaturated terrain with shallow stratified ribs, six low mineral fins, and one mature crystallogenic colony from original asset package 14. No new point lights, particles, map enlargement or repeated sporefield foliage. The original authored model is optional; a complete grounded procedural stand-in remains for restricted quality or failed asset load. The mature model is uniformly fitted to the same collider envelope and does not enter the production asset manifest.

The north-south corridor remains open. Only scatter inside the new bed is removed; the legacy seeded static placement outside it is retained. Ordinary grass cover is suppressed locally (decorative scatter may vary elsewhere). The minimap and zone label identify the location. No enemy, loot, harvest, quests, entry requirements, damage volumes, or implied interactive affordances are added in this foundational increment.

Simulated reviews: world design — differentiate an open mineral ecology from the industrial Brineworks and soft Sporefield; art — layered material silhouettes and non-emissive geology, reuse actual mature colony rather than repeating conventional glowing towers; technical art — one isolated model with deterministic stand-in, no unbounded scatter; simulation — bounded authoritative colliders and walkable corridor, existing enemy and interaction definitions unchanged; adversarial — model-load failure retains collider-matched scenery, finite terrain, route and prop bounds checked; retrospective — establish navigable terrain and habitat before inventing harvest/crafting dependencies. Node tests and a browser capture scenario are added; visual, hardware and hosted results pending at commit. No final art approval.
