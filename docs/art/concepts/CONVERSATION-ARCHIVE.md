# Conversation concept archive — retrospective index
Recovered from design discussions of 16–19 November 2026. This is a textual index, not a copy of original artwork. Verify source files and existing repository assets before importing; do not duplicate or publish restricted material.

## Established art direction
Mature industrial science-fantasy: industrial brutalism, megastructures, utilitarian settlements, underground facilities, cybernetics, biotechnology, heavy worn machinery, alien deserts, toxic zones, decaying cities and altered wilderness. Grounded materials, strong silhouettes and mature ambiguity. Earlier ornate fantasy direction was rejected. Anarchy Online and Project LLL are influences only, not licensed asset sources.
