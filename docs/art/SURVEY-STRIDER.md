# Survey Strider — first ground companion candidate

A Surveyor-specific utility companion, deliberately not a second damage pet or an offtank. Fabricate and equip the Survey Strider Link at commissioned Engineering Station B; unequip outside combat to dismiss. Existing Mender is not altered. Its three commands retain Shift+3, Shift+4 and Shift+5 respectively.

The source rig is the existing CC0 Quaternius walker from the recorded scifi_essentials ledger. Original twin instrument pods and a raised scanning mast are attached to the animated body to communicate an industrial survey purpose. Existing hostile models are unchanged. The original rig's locomotion and Look clip are reused; analysis reuses the actual fault-scan cast/VFX pipeline, not a simulated client-only mechanic. Low-spec fallback is a separately labeled maintenance chassis with a mast. Human visual acceptance, locomotion at all slopes and in-game capture must be verified before claiming finished art.

Design decisions: no attack, threat, tanking, healing, or cells. Analysis is bounded, cannot replace an active player mark, and requires a selected eligible enemy, proximity and clear cover. Dismissal invalidates its own outstanding mark; unowned or invalid equipment cannot deploy. Owner defeat makes it dormant; owner recovery redeploys at a valid footprint. The unit cannot path through walls or teleport to catch up; if stranded, dismiss/re-equip outside combat. This first utility type is not targetable by enemies, consistent with current companion combat constraints. Enemy-targetable defensive ground species and independent HP/death need a subsequent threat/lifecycle design and test.
