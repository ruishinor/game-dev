# Survey Strider — learned companion deployment
Engineering fabrication permanently learns the Survey Strider capability. Existing owned physical links migrate to learned state, releasing the device slot and preserving an active owner's deployment preference. Learning does not auto-summon. Select the default in Companion Sheet and summon using the next available numbered action slot (Surveyor 5), or the sheet button. Dismissal and summon remain server-authoritative, outside combat while alive; reconnect preserves the deployment preference. No new companion may be summoned by a character without the learned capability.
Ground navigation, follow/stay and analysis remain. A successful bounded analysis transfers 15 Power Cells (manual Fault Scan remains stronger at 30), respects range, cover, neutral target protection, active marks and the existing cooldown. No additional direct attack, loot automation or finalized artwork is claimed. The existing CC0 tripod donor, original instrument pods and provenance are unchanged. Visual quality and balance require human review.
