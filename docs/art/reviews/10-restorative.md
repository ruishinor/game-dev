# 10 — Restorative and symbiotic effect library

Four render-only samplers: restore, bond, transfer and stabilize. Source/target space, limited rising motes, distinct link paths and low-opacity prevention dome. No health, threat, ownership, companion or synchronization changes.

Simulated roundtable:
- Art: restrained green/teal and violet signal motifs; no gore or unnecessary energy storms.
- Technical art: stable IDs and shared effect renderer; distinct shapes for area restoration and direct links; no skeletal dependence.
- Runtime: capped mote/beam counts, explicit expiration; bounded source-target distance and no simulation.
- UX: target ring and link geometry imply relationship; colour is supplementary. Reduced motion uses static indicators. Actual allied/hostile context and status text remain client integration concerns.
- Adversarial: zero-distance links and nonfinite vectors rejected, sloped/vertical links supported, no leaked effects after sampled duration. Actual Three disposal tests staged.
- Retrospective: kept visual support separate from authoritative healing and buff rules. No WebGL or animation-fit evidence.

Executed: 168 sampled frame validations; max 7 objects, deterministic replay, invalid inputs and expiration.
