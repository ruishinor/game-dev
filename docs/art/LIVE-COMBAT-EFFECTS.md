# Live combat effect integration

Existing versioned art sampler packages 08–10 are now used directly at authoritative cast/damage/beam/effect events (Hinge, Relay, Phase Needle, Reversal, Core Bore, Rending Arc, Fault Scan, demolition, Kinetic Ward, Discharge, Mending Graft, Mender transfer and Overgrowth). Functional radii and basic effects remain for interpretation and low quality. The authored visual layers are bounded to ten transient effects with explicit cleanup; existing persistent Aegis is separate. No damage timing, effect radius, resource, cooldown or companion authority moves into the client. Short target links are skipped if invalid; reduced-motion options propagated. Art direction: topology matters more than uniform glow. Technical: 10-instance cap, deterministic frame sample, expiry and cleanup tests. Adversarial: avoid zero direction, invalid coordinates or unbounded accumulation; skip authored layer at q=0. Retrospective: these are rendered procedural layered effects, not final bespoke animations or GPU-profiled art. Browser capture and manual artistic review remain required.
