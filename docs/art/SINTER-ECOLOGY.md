# Sinter Flats — migrating mineral ecology (preview increment)

The pale southern bed is not an abandoned combat arena. Crystallogenic colonies bind mineral dust, and Sinter Grazers filter the resulting deposits while crossing an old drainage bed. A Faultstalker shadows the margin where strata fracture; its mineral plates are useful to field engineers. This is local environmental interpretation, not a canonical origin story for the world.

- Three neutral grazers use an offset, repeating migration circuit. Proximity and selection are safe; direct damage (including explicit area attacks) provokes the victim, not the entire herd. No XP or loot for killing a grazer. Harry does not attack a neutral merely because the owner selected it. Neutral name/target labels and minimap markers are distinct; click to target, Tab skips calm fauna.
- One territorial Faultstalker follows its own route, engages on proximity, and telegraphs Fault Shear. It can drop a unique Sinter Laminate device (45%, preliminary tuning): +1 Kinetics, +1 Biotech, +18 HP. No crafting or new inventory schema; Workshop integration remains separate.
- Both new species have dedicated procedural silhouettes, articulated gait and hit/cast/death response rather than recolored Skitters. Existing primitive helpers reused. No new downloads, particles, lights or asset-manifest changes. No final art quality or mobile performance claim.

## Second ecology increment — life stages and herd response

Three neutral individuals now have independent seven/eight-point routes, differentiated travel speeds and feeding pauses. The juvenile is shorter, the adult is a generalist, and the shieldback is broad and armoured. Their circuits intersect and diverge; they are not a synchronized formation. Two completed circuits cause additional mineral ridge growth, exposed via an optional snapshot field so joining clients see the same state. This is bounded within-life phenotypic maturation, not genetic simulation or permanent character progression. Respawn resets it.

Direct player damage triggers a short local alarm; only a nearby shieldback may defend a herd member, while an unengaged non-defender scatters locally. The original victim retains retaliation. Neutral proximity and selection remain safe, no XP/loot changes, no additional enemies or RNG draws. Predator remains independently hostile. Calm overlapping nameplates are suppressed by priority, and health bars remain available on selection or combat. Existing active enemy/object labels are retained. Fault Shear gains an identifiable mineral dust burst; herd alarm, defence and maturation have bounded ring/burst cues with reduced-motion accommodation.

Simulated review: ecology — asynchronous loops and feeding instead of fixed formation; engineering — additive optional snapshot form/state, no durable save change, no extra entity count; economy — no neutral reward, no artificial armour stat buff; art — differentiated carapace, gills, jaws, mineral seams, tail and feeding pose; adversarial — all-waypoint route checks, wall/prop clearance, isolated vs local aggression, observation safe, bounded maturation. Outstanding: browser and player acceptance, actual ecological separation/spacing in camera, predator-prey hunting (not implemented), genetic persistence and production-grade textured assets.

Follow-up: the predator now has a longer stalking/watch circuit. An idle Faultstalker within 4.8m may cause local, time-bounded natural flight and shieldback bracing, without cross-species damage, aggro or rewards. A separate cooldown preserves migration. This is threat avoidance rather than a full predator-prey combat simulation. The shared 64×64 deterministic color texture adds mineral striation across carapace surfaces; a denser shared shell mesh retains existing model geometry conventions. The preview browser checks real material maps and form-specific rigs, and captures controlled matured forms. Node tests verify both spread and convergence, routine separation, foraging, stalking, and natural reaction. Browser evidence is not a subjective art pass.

## Fourth increment — autonomous territorial encounters
Faultstalker now selects nearby idle grazers (vulnerable forms first), crouches, circles, lunges and withdraws on a bounded server timeline. Successful contact causes nonlethal actual grazer HP damage recovered after a delay; a nearby intervening shieldback can repel an attack. Other grazers cover a flank or flee. Player threat supersedes wildlife; no wildlife-driven player damage, XP, drops, death or predator HP manipulation. Existing migration and feeding resume after a cooldown. Simulated review: initial predator selection failed the protection fixture; corrected preference before publication. Contact, intervention, deterministic local geometry, bounded state, preemption and no economy changes are explicit checks. Existing migration-only and maturation-only tests remain independent of hunts. Engine VFX handles pounce/repel and contact. Full persistence, reproduction and lethal predation remain out of scope; native and visual verification pending.

## Fifth increment — habitat and conditional morphology
Two small passable environmental landmarks visualize a sheltered nursery and mineral-rich feeding bed. The server uses the matching site coordinates: nursery pauses are longer and wildlife wounds recover faster inside the shelter; feeding-bed visits accumulate exposure. Mature adult generalists that accumulate at least 14 seconds of mineral feeding develop distinct calcified ridges and process subsequent deposit stops faster. Surviving natural attacks leave visible scars for that life. Optional snapshot flags reveal both forms to all clients without saves, rewards or stats changes. Not genetic inheritance, true breeding, or a spawn-rate claim; the advanced adult phenotype is conditional. Browser evidence covers habitat registration, snapshot-driven appearance and actual defeated target state. Shieldback interception tightens the geometric check to a forward segment corridor. Simulated review: cosmetic landmarks stay inside the existing marine-mineral ecology, no collisions or new harvest nodes; same material resources; controlled fixture tests cannot prove natural frequency or aesthetic quality. 

Retrospective CI finding: habitat-rate healing left a double-precision residue at 84.99999999999999 HP. Normalize only sub-epsilon residue at completion, retain the exact-health regression assertion. Do not heal unrelated combat damage.
