# Field Maintenance Kit

Initial visible utility consumable: one conductive cassette restores up to 25 percentage points of condition on the owner's equipped repairable weapon outside combat. No HP recovery, revival, other-character repair or benefit at full condition. Workshop Engineering produces two from one fibre and one alloy. Quartermaster sells one for 12 credits, provisional; station service remains cheaper. Existing goods persistence and stack cap.

Public presentation: identity, cassette icon, explicit behavior and recipe. Prices and transaction validation are server-owned; public Git source is not confidential. Authenticated intent is checked against current ownership, equipment, combat state, stock, and the session's utility revision; successful craft and use advance the same revision. Stale requests cannot repeat the transaction; vendor quotes use their existing revision. Utility goods are excluded from junk sales.

Restrained amber engineering sigil/contact sparks through CombatVfx; ring fallback. Hosted tests cover authority, purchase, crafting, consumption and UI. Rendered artistic acceptance remains separate from regression results.
