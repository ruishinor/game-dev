# Original Survey Strider — runtime candidate
Independently authored, mechanical three-foot tripod, faceted enamel/metal chassis, actuated two-segment legs, piston links and orientable scanning head. The live model uses original Three.js geometry. It preserves one articulated rig across near and distant detail, switches to simpler geometry beyond 22 metres and returns to full detail below 18 metres. Distant geometry draws fewer meshes and under 60% of the near-view triangles. Model state drives motion and survey/screen indication. Collision and utility remain server-authoritative. Emergency screen is a distinct mode, with a 20-point owner barrier triggered below half health in combat, only with clear range and no active barrier, on a 24-second cooldown. Existing scan support and other companions are preserved.

The [checked-in near and distant GLBs](../../tools/asset-pipeline/assets/survey-strider/outputs/) use the same authored geometry and contain baked Walk and Survey clips. Regenerate and validate them with `node tools/assets/survey-strider.mjs`. The GLBs are available for offline asset inspection. Live gameplay evaluates the articulated rig directly for terrain-independent spacing, movement, scan and recovery. In-context animation and artistic acceptance remain open.

## Salvage recovery utility
Shift+2 commands an active Strider to recover one nearby (18 m owner range) eligible personal corpse loot allocation outside combat. The robot navigates to the remains, enforces range and cover, and reuses the same capacity-checked grant and removal transaction as ordinary looting. No loot is rolled, duplicated or taken from other characters. Recovery is bounded and returns to Follow. The command is transient, not a persistent automatic loot mode.
