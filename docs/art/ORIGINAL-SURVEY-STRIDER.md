# Original Survey Strider — runtime candidate
Independently authored, mechanical three-foot tripod, faceted enamel/metal chassis, actuated two-segment legs, piston links and orientable scanning head. The live model uses general Three.js geometry; no imported donor mesh or claim of a verified GLB export. Model state drives motion and survey/screen indication; collision and actual utility remain authoritative. Emergency screen is a distinct mode, with a 20-point owner barrier triggered below half health in combat, only with clear range and no active barrier, on a 24-second cooldown. Existing scan support and other companions are preserved. Requires visual/animation review, profiling, authored binary asset pipeline integration and balance validation prior to final art acceptance.
