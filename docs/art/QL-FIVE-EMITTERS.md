# QL5 emitter specialization — initial weapon progression candidate

Retain QL1 and QL3 weapons. QL5 Dense Pulse Core prioritizes damage (+6 versus +4); Longpoint Emitter trades damage (+2) for up to 4m additional ranged auto-attack reach. Both consume a carried QL3 emitter and materials at commissioned Engineering station B. Kinetics or Instrumentation 13 and Resonance are required separately for fabrication and equipment. Longpoint uses no Kinetics-gated intermediate, allowing an instrumentation specialist to complete its own path. Both are internal pulse cores for existing class weapons; no external geometry or animation substitution is claimed. Existing weapon attacks, equipment and VFX preserve the existing behavior; Longpoint changes the server's range check but not ability range, cadence, line of sight, or source of projectile events. Condition scales both Longpoint benefits; a depleted weapon does not remove the base attack.

Public player-facing item stats and crafting recipes are intentionally exposed; no hidden reward tables, pricing rules, client authority or payload fields added. The new options are provisional balance choices, not proven optimal. Regression tests cover original range, depleted and unowned states, station/skill gating, atomic conversion and actual range checks. A browser planner assertion is included; new equipment appearance and high-quality effects are not independently accepted.
