# Field Recovery — first field discipline

All four professions may recover loose surface shedding at the established mineral bed in Sinter Flats. No living colony or grazer is harvested, no XP is granted, and existing predator risk, migration and Sinter Laminate acquisition remain unchanged. The existing habitat is the world asset, not a duplicated resource mesh. An interaction anchor, clear prompt, unique inventory icon, modest VFX and Codex entry communicate the action.

Initial ranks: 1 (beginner) and 2 (practitioner, three successful collections). Rank 2 adds an Engineering recipe that produces two existing maintenance cassettes from two loose flakes and one alloy; the ordinary recipe and vendor remain available. A separate persisted per-character replenishment interval avoids both competitive node camping and reconnect resets. Cap checks precede mutation. Progress, cooldown and material grants are server-calculated. Only current owner progress and remaining time are sent to the authenticated character; the recipe's visible threshold is public. The public development repository does not conceal source rules.

This is an intentionally small first discipline, not a finished multi-profession economy. Browser evidence uses the existing terrain asset plus a real F interaction. Actual visual quality and economic pacing require playtesting.
