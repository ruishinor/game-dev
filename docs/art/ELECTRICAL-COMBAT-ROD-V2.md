## Implementation
- Runtime opts into independent channel materials; export retains the shared structural/material mesh contract and Join/Split geometry. Added collar, midshaft and emitter anchors. No new model licence or owner reference file.
- Pooled lightning (up to five primary paths in joined mode, two branches per path), two tip trails, impact sparks (64 joined, 36 dual before engine budget), corona-like flare particles and short cyan pooled flash. Ground ripple is purely decorative, not a combat range indicator; suppressed for shield impacts. Lower quality and calm reduce layers; reduced motion disables complex effects; quality zero keeps subdued mesh emission. Default idle pulses are small, no permanent light. Per-weapon cooldowns plus existing global pool ceilings bound emission; removal/culling cancels controller handles; emitted particles expire naturally.
- Live effects gated by visibility, camera distance, equipment and fabrication/death presentation. No dedicated rig animations, true two-handed constraints or authored pre-hit windup are claimed.
- Preview adds charge, strike, impact, repeat, quality and intensity controls. Inspect dual/join, reversal, effect tiers, calm/power and close-up motion. Standalone URL: /client/electrical-rod-preview.html.

## V3 — persistent corona, combat escalation and stowing — 2026-11-20

Candidate on `feat/vanguard-ranged-slot-rod-vfx`. Presentation only: damage, reach, attack interval, condition and price are unchanged; contact effects still fire only from confirmed server damage events.

- **At rest.** The weapon-local corona (`client/src/vfx/rod-corona.js`) now layers an additive plasma sheath, an emitter glow sprite per half and flickering crackle strands (six prebuilt jagged variants per half, two to four shown at 10–26 Hz) over the original helical filaments. It allocates nothing per frame and uses no shared particle, ribbon or light pool, so it cannot starve ability effects. Combat raises the idle floor; joined burns hotter than dual.
- **Phases.** `rod.pulse()` escalates every local layer for about half a second. Transform, draw (unstowing), ability charge, strike and contact pulse it; the shared engine adds bounded arcs, radial discharge spokes (joined 3, dual 2), denser sparks (joined 96, dual 56 before the engine budget), a brief light pulse (pooled flash at quality 2; a bright flare plus emissive surge at quality 1, where the engine keeps no lights) and residual crackle (one small arc at a time for 0.8–1.2 s). Strike timeline stages were compressed (0 / 0.06 / 0.14 s) with residual stages at 0.32 and 0.58 s. In-hand rod strikes play 1.3× faster with a shorter fade (presentation only).
- **Gating.** Reduced motion: static sheath and glow, no crackle, rotation or engine layers. Calm flashes / minimal intensity: no pulses, flashes, spokes, trails or ripple. Quality 0: subdued filament and glow only. Per-weapon cooldowns and handle bounds are retained; budgets are exported as `ROD_VFX`.
- **Stowed.** While the ranged weapon is active the joined rod is carried diagonally across the back (`spine_03`), unlit, and keeps its saved configuration. Drawing it plays a draw discharge.

Evidence: `test/e2e/weapon-switch.mjs` captures idle joined/dual, transform, stowed, ranged fire, dual and joined contact and residual crackle under software GL. No real-GPU performance or owner visual acceptance is claimed.
