Stacked candidate: follows PR #116. No server changes, new data disclosure, item/stat changes, stun or area damage. Existing authority, persistence, region-scoped snapshots and Longpoint presentation remain intact. Only existing confirmed melee auto damage events from an equipped Vanguard (er 1/2) can trigger a live contact discharge; ordinary ability effects and fallback melee impact remain in place. ## Implementation
- Runtime opts into independent channel materials; export retains the shared structural/material mesh contract and Join/Split geometry. Added collar, midshaft and emitter anchors. No new model licence or owner reference file.
- Pooled lightning (up to five primary paths in joined mode, two branches per path), two tip trails, impact sparks (64 joined, 36 dual before engine budget), corona-like flare particles and short cyan pooled flash. Ground ripple is purely decorative, not a combat range indicator; suppressed for shield impacts. Lower quality and calm reduce layers; reduced motion disables complex effects; quality zero keeps subdued mesh emission. Default idle pulses are small, no permanent light. Per-weapon cooldowns plus existing global pool ceilings bound emission; removal/culling cancels controller handles; emitted particles expire naturally.
- Live effects gated by visibility, camera distance, equipment and fabrication/death presentation. No dedicated rig animations, true two-handed constraints or authored pre-hit windup are claimed.
- Preview adds charge, strike, impact, repeat, quality and intensity controls. Inspect dual/join, reversal, effect tiers, calm/power and close-up motion. Standalone URL: /client/electrical-rod-preview.html.
