# Severance Expanse — environment direction

Owner-approved direction, 2026-11-19. Concept and delivery brief, not implementation evidence.

A dedicated rift leads to an outdoor arid alien landscape, with a visible monumental Foundry entrance at a distance. Arrival and return remain safe. Players traverse the landscape before entering a separately bounded dungeon. Preserve Phase Annex and existing rifts. Anarchy Online Temple of Three Winds is a reference for destination identity and approach pacing only; use original assets and layout.

The owner-supplied entrance concept specifies a tapered dark stone monolith, stepped base and ramp, deeply recessed nested archways, carved channels and restrained amber glyph inscriptions. Reuse existing entrance asset work if compatible rather than duplicating it. The doorway must be traversable or explicitly transition to the dungeon; no invisible collision across the entry path.

Outdoor visual direction: weathered rock, distant ridges, dry terrain, mineral/crystal formations, broken monoliths, sparse hostile patrols and legible navigation. Avoid excessive emissive glow. Keep safe arrival and return routes available. Interior direction: industrial fabrication, biological cultivation, resonance machinery and mineral excavation. Original enemy silhouettes and combat behaviors; armor fabrication progression is a separate integration dependency. Do not publish undiscovered triggers, private encounter scripts or hidden loot odds.

Delivery order: reconcile concurrent entrance and rift work; bounded outdoor map and safe round-trip transition; integrate original entrance with collision and scale; populate outdoor encounters and environmental props; dungeon transition and interior encounters; connect authoritative recipe acquisition only after armor crafting contracts are available. Validate location, collision, death/reconnect, party behavior, combat authority and regressions. Actual visual review and performance measurements remain separate gates.

## Expedition Outpost

Owner requested a vendor area in the outdoor Expanse with purchasable healing equivalents. Place a safe expedition camp between the arrival rift and Foundry, off the main approach but clearly visible. The supplied shelter references depict a low angular roof, exposed triangulated trusses, ochre fabric side panels, dark composite edging, ground anchors and an accessible entrance. Build original geometry through the existing asset pipeline; use authored mesh, UVs, PBR materials and appropriate LOD/collision, not a claim of photogrammetry from concept sheets. Static shelter needs no skeletal rig unless actual moving parts justify it. Furnish the interior and verify character and camera clearance.

Proposed services: expedition quartermaster (required), field technician and researcher (optional later). Reuse existing NPC/vendor/repair contracts. Stock class-neutral immediate health restoration and optionally gradual/stronger restoration, repair kits and Surveyor Power Cells where supported. Prefer existing consumables over duplicate mechanics; authoritative prices, ownership, capacity, cooldown, consumption, bounded healing, dead-character rejection and persistence; validate repeated requests and concurrent purchase/use. No hidden rates or unreleased assets in public documentation. Keep safe-zone behavior consistent with combat, arrival and return. Verify vendor access and purchase/use across reconnect and all active classes. No implementation or visual acceptance claimed by this brief.
