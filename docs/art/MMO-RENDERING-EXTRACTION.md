# Reference-scene engineering decisions

References are pinned to inspected source. File links point to the revisions used for the comparison. Attunement keeps its Three.js client and authoritative server. This pass uses the existing GLB derivatives and does not copy external implementation code.

| Concern | External implementation and maturity | Attunement equivalent | Technique and action | Benefit, risk and verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scene hierarchy and appearance | Experimental [VanillaGL `model-loader.ts`, 6e188ce](https://github.com/AlinV2V/VanillaGL/blob/6e188ce/apps/client/src/model-loader.ts) assembles parts over shared geometry leases and mounts equipment at named joints. MIT. | `models.js`, `looks.js` and `gear-kit.js` build dressed per-entity rigs and attach equipment to bones. `main.js` consumes snapshots. | Retain Attunement's separate appearance and class, per-entity material overrides, and shared rig. Defer new body plans and racial mechanics. | Existing saves and attachment offsets remain compatible. Browser playtest checks independent looks, rigged movement and remote poses. |
| Asset preparation, caching and lifetime | VanillaGL [`setup-assets.ts`](https://github.com/AlinV2V/VanillaGL/blob/6e188ce/tools/setup-assets.ts) executes required bake stages, validates outputs and emits hashes. MIT. Wowser Scene 0.32.0 [`ModelManager.ts`, cbe1211](https://github.com/wowserhq/scene/blob/cbe1211/src/lib/model/ModelManager.ts) caches pending and loaded resources separately. MIT. | `tools/assets/build.mjs` already bakes GLB with glTF Transform, WebP and Meshopt. `assets.js` coalesces requests, shares GPU resources and clones skeletons. The server already serves GLB and allowlists the decoder. | Retain formats and loader. Verify declared kit pieces and clips before a bundle enters the ready cache. | A decoded incomplete kit can't leave a half-built world. Validate real bundles and verify missing-bundle fallback. No new encoder or browser dependency. |
| Animation | VanillaGL [`m2-animator.ts`, 6e188ce](https://github.com/AlinV2V/VanillaGL/blob/6e188ce/apps/client/src/m2-animator.ts) resolves sequence variations and samples bones and attachment transforms. MIT, experimental. | `animator.js` uses Three.js AnimationMixer for locomotion, crossfades, action overlays and death. `anim-tables.js` maps outcomes to stock clips. | Retain GLB clips, shared skeleton naming and state mapping. Defer format-specific GPU skinning and foreign-rig retargeting. | Model or animation timing has no authority over damage. Test both observers, companion modes, enemy hit reactions and respawn. |
| Visibility | Older Wowser Scene [`DoodadManager.ts`, cbe1211](https://github.com/wowserhq/scene/blob/cbe1211/src/lib/map/DoodadManager.ts) culls an area by a sphere, then tests individual doodads. MIT. | `scene.js` already groups static placements by geometry, material and cell in InstancedMesh batches. | Retain the two levels of batching and culling. Group new flora into local colonies and route-side bands. | A bounded decorative population can establish habitat structure without multiplying draw calls. Compare actual submissions, density, and camera views. |
| Streaming | Active Benilla [`spatial_cache.rs`, 84022ba](https://github.com/samwhosung/benilla/blob/84022ba/crates/benilla-assets/src/spatial_cache.rs) expires entries after both geographic and idle thresholds. Wenilla [`web_budget.rs`, ba8ab06](https://github.com/Arnesen/wenilla/blob/ba8ab06/crates/benilla-world/src/terrain_stream/collider/web_budget.rs) budgets browser collider construction and discards canceled jobs. Both MIT or Apache-2.0. Roblox documents [atomic model residency](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/streaming). | One small world, assets loaded once, bounded in-memory cache, no streamed collision. | Keep cohesive static bundles. Defer eviction, progressive world streaming and any separate physics loader until profiling justifies them. | Avoid visible reload churn. Measure draw calls and texture/geometry counts at fixed viewpoints. |
| Server cells | [AzerothCore `GridNotifiers.cpp`, 6ffb62a](https://github.com/azerothcore/azerothcore-wotlk/blob/6ffb62a/src/server/game/Grids/Notifiers/GridNotifiers.cpp) handles nearby object/visibility notifications. [TrinityCore `ObjectGridLoader.cpp`, 36ae56e](https://github.com/TrinityCore/TrinityCore/blob/36ae56e/src/server/game/Grids/ObjectGridLoader.cpp) gates grid spawns and handles phased loading. [vMaNGOS `ObjectGridLoader.cpp`, 70e38cd](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/70e38cd/src/game/ObjectGridLoader.cpp) returns displaced creatures to their original grid at eviction. Inspected source headers use GPL-2.0-or-later. | `shared/world.js` and `server/sim/` own world state and collision. | Retain existing server, collision, and snapshots. Only client decorative residency would be a future candidate. | No networking, settlement, reward or movement changes. Run Node and two-context browser checks. |
| Camera and composition | VanillaGL [`camera-zoom.ts`](https://github.com/AlinV2V/VanillaGL/blob/6e188ce/apps/client/src/player/camera-zoom.ts) and [`movement-controller.ts`](https://github.com/AlinV2V/VanillaGL/blob/6e188ce/apps/client/src/player/movement-controller.ts) separate requested radius from collision-adjusted placement. Wowser [`BaseOrbitControls.ts`](https://github.com/wowserhq/scene/blob/cbe1211/src/lib/controls/BaseOrbitControls.ts) provides orbit controls, with no comparable gameplay obstruction solver in that class. MIT. | `camera.js` already keeps desired and placed radius. `scene.js` tests nine points along each player/target view ray. | Keep requested zoom and exact-endpoint collision; intersect the whole sightline against existing visual volumes. Preserve original material settings after fade. Use an open approach with canopy mass off the route. | Thin blockers can no longer fall between samples. Check near/tangent/missing blockers, foliage transitions, targeted combat views and default pointer lock. |

The [Ashes of Creation Alpha preview](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4124950/Ashes_of_Creation/) and [NCSOFT's Cinder City announcement](https://about.ncsoft.com/en/news/article/cc_update_250818) are visual references for framing and recognizable industrial landmarks. Their cited material does not expose game source. Neither supplied an asset or an engine dependency.

Quaternius and Poly Haven are already licensed and in the build ledger. Reviewed alternatives include [Quaternius Animated Alien Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedalien.html), [Kenney Modular Space Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/modular-space-kit), [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/faq), and [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com/). The first two offer CC0 candidates. The existing kits already share the scene's materials and rigs, so this pass uses those assets. An OpenGameArt listing needs an asset-specific license check. Mixamo was not downloaded or used.
