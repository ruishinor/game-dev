## Editorial principles

People have jobs, needs, disagreements and limited knowledge. Avoid generic chosen-one plot, repetitive kill quotas and mandatory grind. Preserve current canon, named NPCs and player choices. Do not publish actual undiscovered triggers, encounter scripts or hidden loot odds in public files.
